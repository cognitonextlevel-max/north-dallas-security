# Design Rationale - Elite B2B Physical Security

## Overview
North Dallas Security & Protective Services (NDSPS) is a premier physical security guard contractor. The design rejects "gaming slop" and "AI template" aesthetics in favor of a level-10, agency-grade layout (Clay/Digital Silk). It pairs high-contrast editorial typography (Cormorant Garamond italic serifs + Inter sans-serifs) with fine-line structural borders, subtle glassmorphic panels, and a custom slate-grid background mesh.

## Key Guidelines
1. **Larry McEnerney Copywriting:** The entire copywriting focuses on the reader (DFW property managers, logistics operators, HOAs). We start with instability (guard liabilities, vendor babysitting), highlight the hidden financial/legal costs of the status quo (lack of DAR reports, guard guesswork), present vetted services as the solution, and conclude with a low-risk 2-4 week pilot call-to-action.
2. **Premium Grid Mesh Background:** Standard flat backgrounds are replaced with a high-end, two-tone slate grid and radial gold ambient glow, creating visual depth and agency texture.
3. **Physical Guard Focus:** The company provides physical guard operations ONLY (armed, unarmed, mobile patrols). No camera systems, tech integrations, or alarms are offered.
4. **Micro-Interactions:** Buttons use smooth sliding fill effects on hover. Cards transition seamlessly using thin gold borders and elevation translations.
